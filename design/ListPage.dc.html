Horror Movies That Actually Hold Up

Rewatched all of these in October with people who had never seen them. These are the five where nobody reached for their phone.

Ranked·5 items·Aug 30, 2026
01
The Thing The practical effects still land because they are actually there. Nothing has aged into camp.
02
Rosemary's Baby Almost no gore and it is still the most upsetting thing on this list.
03
Halloween Everything the sequels did wrong is a thing this one deliberately does not do.
04
Jaws Broken shark, better movie. The restraint was an accident and it worked.
05
Night of the Living Dead Public domain, so everyone has seen a bad transfer. Find a good one.
